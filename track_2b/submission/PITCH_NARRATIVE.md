# TenderProof pitch narrative

Infrastructure contractors make expensive equipment commitments across customer specifications, supplier documents and acceptance evidence. TenderProof keeps those facts attached to one reviewed package so purchasing and shipment decisions cannot silently outlive their supporting evidence.

The implemented workbench has requirements review, supplier comparison, acceptance and change history. Apertus proposes structured interpretations; deterministic rules check supported quantities, applicable evidence and price/delivery bases. Authorized reviewers control approval of the exact snapshot. Content changes stale prior decisions. Incomplete extraction stays a release blocker.

The demonstrated public GPA transformer case contains genuine but mixed-quality Apertus output. A source-cited correction fixes a lot-total/unit-price confusion while retaining the original model value. Missing FAT and unit identity remain unknown. Nothing is approved or shipped. This is a transparent engineering-review prototype, not a claim of automated certification.

The proposed commercial entry is a design partner's transformer package with a named review owner and paid deployment. Measure missed obligations, correction effort and deployment cost against an expert baseline. Expand across equipment categories and enterprise integrations only when those results support purchase. The twenty extensions in the deck and roadmap are future work.

The deck uses ten core slides and two appendices: problem, four workspaces, decision mechanism, real source/correction, actual model output, acceptance gaps, on-premise architecture, commercial test, measured results, extension roadmap and assistant source-reading appendix. Speaker notes carry source attribution and limitations.
